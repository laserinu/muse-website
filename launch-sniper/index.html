Launch Sniper

A launch-driven trader for every EVM chain, now live on Polygon, Arc, Base and Robinhood. It watches for newly created tokens, buys only the ones it can prove it can also sell, and sells immediately — one round trip per launch. 20 round trips completed so far.

01

How it works

Three stages per cycle, in this order. A token that fails any stage is skipped — there is no override.

1 · Detect the launch

Three sources feed it: onchain scanning for new Uniswap V4 pools as they are created (Initialize events), plus the Bankr and Clanker launch feeds, live since 12 Nov 2026, 02:41 GMT+7. Feed launches pass through exactly the same simulation, gas guard, quotas and caps as onchain finds. On the fork test the scanner found 24 new V4 pools in the last 50,000 Polygon blocks.

2 · Simulate both legs

The one gate that matters: a full buy and sell are simulated before any real transaction. Buyable-but-not-sellable tokens are excluded by definition — dead and empty pools are rejected here.

3 · Buy, sell, log

Exact-amount approvals, one buy, one immediate sell. Gas and the buy–sell shortfall are charged against the quota and written to the trip log — the log, not any website, is the record of what each trip cost.

05

Fixes and hardening — 12 Nov 2026

A day of live trading surfaced real faults; each was root-caused and fixed the same day. This is the honest list, including the one item still open.

Runner fix batch — landed ~03:00 GMT+7Balance-read retries, receipt-read tolerance, and atomic state locking, so a transient read failure can no longer burn a launch or corrupt the trip accounting.
DEADLIFT sell root cause found and fixedThe sell bundle's approve transaction reverted because that token fixes its Permit2 allowance at infinity — the redundant approve is now dropped whenever the allowance already covers the sell. The position itself was sold manually by the owner on 11 Nov 2026, 23:54 GMT+7, after the runner's sell failed.
Pre-buy balance gate — fixed 04:20 GMT+7Wrapped-native balance now counts toward the gate, matching what the buy path can actually spend.
Trip recording — fixed 05:09 GMT+7Trip amounts now record their true values even when the external price feed is unreachable.
Arc balance read — fixed later that morningNative-paired Arc pools quote through the zero address, and the balance check now handles that case. Proven live the same day: Arc's 10 round trips.
Open item: signing service intermittently throws on broadcastIt happened 5 times on 12 Nov. No funds were lost in any of them — a failed broadcast simply means the launch is missed. A bounded one-time retry for exactly this case is being added.

Rollout

Scope is every EVM chain — but a chain only goes live once it is verified: a working RPC, the factory or PoolManager address, a proven buy-and-sell route, and gas economics that leave room after the round trip. Solana is a separate build, later.

07

The build

Thirteen files under tokens/sniper/launch/ — the only sniper now running.

  • runnermain loop, one round trip per launch
  • detectiononchain V4 Initialize scanning
  • Bankr feedlaunch detection from the Bankr feed
  • Clanker feedlaunch detection from the Clanker feed
  • simbuy + sell simulation gate
  • gas guardper-trip and per-day gas budgets
  • chain registryverified chains and their contracts
  • configper-chain buy size and caps, reloadable
  • statequota and trip accounting
  • state-edit helpersafe, locked edits to the state file
  • fork testchecks on a local mainnet copy
  • READMEdesign and operating notes
  • kill switchhalts everything, proven in dry run

The Robinhood testnet loop is complete: 976 round trips, its 0.005 test quota filled on 12 Nov 2026, 03:23 GMT+7, and the loop is closed. The earlier v2 and v3 snipers were retired and archived on 12 Nov 2026, ~12:52 GMT+7 — this launch sniper is now the only sniper running.
