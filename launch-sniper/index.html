01

How it works

Three stages per launch, in this order. A token that fails any stage is skipped — there is no override.

1 · Detect the launch

An always-on watcher follows Arc block by block and picks up new Uniswap V4 pools as they are created (Initialize events) — seconds after the birth block, not at the next scheduled check. The Bankr and Clanker launch feeds, live since 12 Nov 2026, 02:41 GMT+7, feed the same pipeline. Every launch passes through exactly the same simulation and gas guard, whichever source found it. On the fork test the scanner found 24 new V4 pools in the last 50,000 Polygon blocks.

2 · Simulate both legs

The one gate that matters: a full buy and sell are simulated before any real transaction. Buyable-but-not-sellable tokens are excluded by definition — dead and empty pools are rejected here.

3 · Buy, sell, log

On Arc the whole trip is one call to the worker contract: it buys and sells back in a single transaction, capped at 0.12 per call, with the minimum return enforced onchain — a token that cannot be sold back never leaves a position behind. Gas and the buy–sell shortfall are written to the trip log — the log, not any website, is the record of what each trip cost.

05

Fixes and hardening — 12 Nov 2026

A day of live trading surfaced real faults; each was root-caused and fixed the same day. This is the honest list — nothing left open.

Runner fix batch — landed ~03:00 GMT+7Balance-read retries, receipt-read tolerance, and atomic state locking, so a transient read failure can no longer burn a launch or corrupt the trip accounting.
DEADLIFT sell root cause found and fixedThe sell bundle's approve transaction reverted because that token fixes its Permit2 allowance at infinity — the redundant approve is now dropped whenever the allowance already covers the sell. The position itself was sold manually by the owner on 11 Nov 2026, 23:54 GMT+7, after the runner's sell failed.
Pre-buy balance gate — fixed 04:20 GMT+7Wrapped-native balance now counts toward the gate, matching what the buy path can actually spend.
Trip recording — fixed 05:09 GMT+7Trip amounts now record their true values even when the external price feed is unreachable.
Arc balance read — fixed later that morningNative-paired Arc pools quote through the zero address, and the balance check now handles that case. Proven live the same day: Arc's 10 round trips.
Send-step failures root-caused: MinOutBuyWorker attempts kept dying at the send step with an unknown-reason revert. Replaying the exact failed attempts onchain decoded it: every one reverted MinOutBuy — a fresh pool's price moves several percent within seconds, past the old minimum-out buffer of 98% of the quoted price. Nothing was wrong with the worker contract, the cap or the signing service, and no funds were lost in any failure.
Fix applied 12 Nov 2026: minimum-out buffer widened to 90%At 90% of the quoted price, all the replayed failures would have executed; the sell leg still guarantees at least half the buy amount back inside the same transaction. Verified by the worker fork suite, 29/29, and by a control run on the original code proving the remaining full-suite differences were fork-window drift, not the change.
