01

How it works

Three stages per cycle, in this order. A token that fails any stage is skipped — there is no override.

1 · Detect the launch

Watches for new pools as they are created — Uniswap V4 first, then V3 and V2, then launchpads. On the fork test it found 24 new V4 pools in the last 50,000 Polygon blocks.

2 · Simulate both legs

The one gate that matters: a full buy and sell are simulated before any real transaction. Buyable-but-not-sellable tokens are excluded by definition — dead and empty pools are rejected here.

3 · Buy, sell, log

Exact-amount approvals, one buy, one immediate sell. Gas and the buy–sell shortfall are charged against the quota and written to the trip log — the log, not any website, is the record of what each trip cost.

02

Per-chain settings

Every chain has its own token, its own price and its own gas — so every number below is per chain, and changing one never touches another. All of it lives in config and can be changed at any time, with no rebuild. Tap a chain to load it into the estimator.

05

Rollout

Scope is every EVM chain — but a chain only goes live once it is verified: a working RPC, the factory or PoolManager address, a proven buy-and-sell route, and gas economics that leave room after the round trip. Solana is a separate build, later.

✓

Fork test

Full flow proven on a local copy of mainnet. 13 of 16 checks passed, failures documented above.

–

Dry run — extended watch skipped

Zero-transaction behaviour was proven in the fork test (the kill switch held), but the planned 24–48 hour watch was skipped by direct go-ahead for this trial.

The build

Ten files under tokens/sniper/launch/ — a separate build from the earlier testnet sniper, which is untouched.

  • runnermain loop, one round trip per launch
  • detectionV4 first, then V3 / V2, then launchpads
  • simbuy + sell simulation gate
  • gas guardper-trip and per-day gas budgets
  • chain registryverified chains and their contracts
  • configper-chain buy size and caps, reloadable
  • statequota and trip accounting
  • fork test16 checks on a local mainnet copy
  • READMEdesign and operating notes
  • kill switchhalts everything, proven in dry run

The older testnet sniper on Robinhood Chain testnet (46630) keeps running as its own build — this one does not replace it.
