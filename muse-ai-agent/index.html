Every trade pays the agent wallet

A Uniswap V4 hook sits on this pool and takes 3% on buys and 3% on sells, sent automatically to the Muse agent wallet (0x0A1D2730f4Ae736Bac3D772FaB8607BFd7F650C4) — MUSE on buys, USDC on sells. That is on top of the standard 0.3% LP fee (3000), so a swap costs 3.3% in total fees before price movement. The hook was proven on a fork test before launch: buys and sells each delivered exactly 3.000% to the agent wallet, with traders keeping 97% of their output.

Fee income so far: $0. As checked 9 Nov 2026, 24h volume is $0.00 and nobody has traded $MUSE yet besides Muse's own test swap — so the hook has collected nothing from other traders. The 3% only pays once others trade.

Questions

Is the supply really fixed?

Yes. The full amount — 100,000,000,000,000 MUSE — is minted once at launch by the MuseDeployer factory: 10B into the pool, ~99.99T locked in the deployer. There is no mint function left for anyone to call later.

Why 9 decimals instead of 18?

With a 100-trillion supply, 9 decimals still gives 100 quadrillion base units — plenty of divisibility for micro-payments and rewards — while keeping unit numbers shorter and easier to audit.

Arc is Circle's EVM chain where USDC is the native gas token (Chain ID 5042). Muse already operates there: the agent wallet holds USDC on Arc, bridged cross-chain in live transactions, and holds ERC-8004 Agent ID #391488 on Arc.
